US$300 salary in Zimbabwe: US$248.08 take-home

A gross monthly salary of US$300 becomes US$248.08 after PAYE, the AIDS levy and NSSA. Here is the full working.

  • USD table
  • You keep 82.7%
  • US$2,976.96 a year

How it is worked out

  1. NSSA is 4.5% of your full pay: US$13.50.
  2. Taxable income: US$300.00 − US$13.50 = US$286.50.
  3. PAYE: US$286.50 × 20% − US$20.00 = US$37.30.
  4. AIDS levy: 3% × US$37.30 = US$1.12.
  5. Take-home: US$300.00 − US$13.50 − US$37.30 − US$1.12 = US$248.08.

Frequently asked questions

What is the take-home pay on a US$300 salary in Zimbabwe?

After NSSA, PAYE and the AIDS levy, a gross monthly salary of US$300 leaves US$248.08 (82.7% of gross), or US$2,976.96 a year. This assumes the employee is under 65, with no pension contributions, tax credits or other deductions.

How much PAYE is paid on US$300 a month?

Taxable income is US$286.50 after NSSA. In the USD table that falls in the 20% band, so PAYE is US$37.30 and the 3% AIDS levy is US$1.12, a total of US$38.42.

Can I change the assumptions?

Yes. Open the calculator with this salary filled in, then add medical aid, pension contributions, credits or other deductions under "More options".
