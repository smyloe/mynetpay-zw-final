ZiG 15,000 salary in Zimbabwe: ZiG 11,645.71 take-home

A gross monthly salary of ZiG 15,000 becomes ZiG 11,645.71 after PAYE, the AIDS levy and NSSA. Here is the full working.

  • ZWG table
  • You keep 77.6%
  • ZiG 139,748.52 a year

How it is worked out

  1. NSSA is 4.5% of your full pay: ZiG 675.00.
  2. Taxable income: ZiG 15,000.00 − ZiG 675.00 = ZiG 14,325.00.
  3. PAYE: ZiG 14,325.00 × 25% − ZiG 980.00 = ZiG 2,601.25.
  4. AIDS levy: 3% × ZiG 2,601.25 = ZiG 78.04.
  5. Take-home: ZiG 15,000.00 − ZiG 675.00 − ZiG 2,601.25 − ZiG 78.04 = ZiG 11,645.71.

ZiG results use a reference exchange rate of ZiG 26.56 per US$1 only to convert the US$700 NSSA ceiling.

Frequently asked questions

What is the take-home pay on a ZiG 15,000 salary in Zimbabwe?

After NSSA, PAYE and the AIDS levy, a gross monthly salary of ZiG 15,000 leaves ZiG 11,645.71 (77.6% of gross), or ZiG 139,748.52 a year. This assumes the employee is under 65, with no pension contributions, tax credits or other deductions.

How much PAYE is paid on ZiG 15,000 a month?

Taxable income is ZiG 14,325.00 after NSSA. In the ZWG table that falls in the 25% band, so PAYE is ZiG 2,601.25 and the 3% AIDS levy is ZiG 78.04, a total of ZiG 2,679.29.

Can I change the assumptions?

Yes. Open the calculator with this salary filled in, then add medical aid, pension contributions, credits or other deductions under "More options".
