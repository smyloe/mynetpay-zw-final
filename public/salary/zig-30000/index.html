ZiG 30,000 salary in Zimbabwe: ZiG 22,603.28 take-home

A gross monthly salary of ZiG 30,000 becomes ZiG 22,603.28 after PAYE, the AIDS levy and NSSA. Here is the full working.

  • ZWG table
  • You keep 75.3%
  • ZiG 271,239.36 a year

How it is worked out

  1. NSSA is 4.5% of the ceiling (US$700 at ZiG 26.56 = ZiG 18,592.00): ZiG 836.64.
  2. Taxable income: ZiG 30,000.00 − ZiG 836.64 = ZiG 29,163.36.
  3. PAYE: ZiG 29,163.36 × 30% − ZiG 2,380.00 = ZiG 6,369.01.
  4. AIDS levy: 3% × ZiG 6,369.01 = ZiG 191.07.
  5. Take-home: ZiG 30,000.00 − ZiG 836.64 − ZiG 6,369.01 − ZiG 191.07 = ZiG 22,603.28.

ZiG results use a reference exchange rate of ZiG 26.56 per US$1 only to convert the US$700 NSSA ceiling.

Frequently asked questions

What is the take-home pay on a ZiG 30,000 salary in Zimbabwe?

After NSSA, PAYE and the AIDS levy, a gross monthly salary of ZiG 30,000 leaves ZiG 22,603.28 (75.3% of gross), or ZiG 271,239.36 a year. This assumes the employee is under 65, with no pension contributions, tax credits or other deductions.

How much PAYE is paid on ZiG 30,000 a month?

Taxable income is ZiG 29,163.36 after NSSA. In the ZWG table that falls in the 30% band, so PAYE is ZiG 6,369.01 and the 3% AIDS levy is ZiG 191.07, a total of ZiG 6,560.08.

Can I change the assumptions?

Yes. Open the calculator with this salary filled in, then add medical aid, pension contributions, credits or other deductions under "More options".
