Where the numbers come from

Every rate in these calculators is taken from official sources, not copied from other calculators: the ZIMRA PAYE tax tables, ZIMRA's PAYE guidance and NSSA's contributions page. USD and ZiG each use their own official table. See how it works. Last checked 18 November 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my payslip different from your result?

Common reasons: taxable allowances or benefits that are in or out of gross pay, a different NSSA ceiling, medical or other tax credits, pension contributions, a bonus or back pay in the same month, or part of your pay in ZiG. Use the payslip checker on the payslip explainer page to see which line differs.

Which exchange rate do you use for ZiG?

ZiG pay is taxed with the ZiG tables, so no conversion is needed for ordinary ZiG salaries. A rate is used only where the law is written in US$: the NSSA ceiling, the bonus limit, the elderly credit and mixed USD and ZiG pay. The default is the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe average interbank rate (26.56 on 11 November 2026). You can change it in any calculator.

What is the tax-free threshold in Zimbabwe?

The first US$100 of monthly taxable income (US$1,200 a year) is taxed at 0% in the USD table, and the first ZiG 2,800 a month (ZiG 33,600 a year) is taxed at 0% in the ZWG table. This is simply the 0% band. It is not an extra credit on top, so a worker earning a little over US$100 a month does pay some PAYE.
