NSSA contributions in plain English

NSSA, the National Social Security Authority, runs the Pension and Other Benefits Scheme. Both you and your employer pay in. Each side pays 4.5% of your insurable earnings, which makes 9% in total going to NSSA on your behalf.

Insurable earnings are capped at US$700 a month (or the ZiG equivalent). That means the most an employee can pay is 4.5% × US$700 = US$31.50 a month, and the employer pays the same. NSSA says the ceiling is gazetted quarterly, so it can change. Contributions are deducted for employees below age 65.

NSSA and your tax

Your NSSA contribution comes off your pay before PAYE is worked out, so it lowers your taxable income a little. The take-home pay calculator does this for you.

Where the numbers come from

Every rate in these calculators is taken from official sources, not copied from other calculators: the ZIMRA PAYE tax tables, ZIMRA's PAYE guidance and NSSA's contributions page. USD and ZiG each use their own official table. See how it works. Last checked 18 November 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How much is NSSA in Zimbabwe?

Under the Pension and Other Benefits Scheme the employee pays 4.5% and the employer pays 4.5% of insurable earnings. Earnings are capped at a ceiling of US$700 a month (or the ZiG equivalent), so the most an employee pays is US$31.50 a month. NSSA says the ceiling is gazetted quarterly, and contributions are deducted for employees below age 65.

Is NSSA taken off before or after tax?

Before. Your employee NSSA contribution is an allowable deduction, so it reduces the income on which PAYE is worked out. That is why a calculator that skips NSSA will show slightly too much PAYE.

Which exchange rate do you use for ZiG?

ZiG pay is taxed with the ZiG tables, so no conversion is needed for ordinary ZiG salaries. A rate is used only where the law is written in US$: the NSSA ceiling, the bonus limit, the elderly credit and mixed USD and ZiG pay. The default is the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe average interbank rate (26.56 on 11 November 2026). You can change it in any calculator.
