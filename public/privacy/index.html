Privacy policy

Short version: your salary stays on your device.

What happens to the numbers you enter

All calculations run in your web browser using JavaScript that is downloaded with the page. The amounts you type are not sent to us, not stored in a database and not shared with anyone.

Links that contain your figures

As you type, the page address updates so you can bookmark or share your results. Those figures are in the link itself. If you copy the link and send it to someone, they will see the numbers. Do not share a link you want to keep private.

Cookies, accounts and tracking

This site does not use cookies, does not ask you to create an account and does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

Hosting

The website files are served by a hosting provider (currently Netlify). Like any web host, it may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and pages requested, for security and to keep the service running. That is separate from anything you enter into the calculators.

Changes

If this changes, for example if analytics are added later, this page will be updated first. Last updated 18 November 2026.
