About MyNetPay ZW and how it works

A free, independent Zimbabwe pay calculator built around official ZIMRA and NSSA figures.

Why this site exists

Zimbabweans are paid in US$, in ZiG, or in a mix of both, and the rules for each are different. MyNetPay ZW exists to give a clear answer to a simple question: how much of my salary will I actually receive? It is free, needs no account and keeps your figures on your own device.

How the calculation works

  1. Gross pay is the monthly figure you enter (basic salary plus taxable allowances).
  2. NSSA employee contribution = 4.5% of gross pay, capped at insurable earnings of US$700 a month (the ZiG ceiling is US$700 converted at the exchange rate shown). Not applied if you are 65 or over.
  3. Taxable income = gross pay minus NSSA minus any approved pension contribution.
  4. PAYE = taxable income × band rate − band deduct, using ZIMRA's monthly USD table for US$ pay and ZIMRA's monthly ZWG table for ZiG pay. Each currency uses its own official table; one is never converted into the other.
  5. Tax credits (medical aid, elderly/blind/disabled) are subtracted from the PAYE, but cannot take it below zero.
  6. AIDS levy = 3% of the PAYE payable. Take-home pay = gross pay minus NSSA, pension, PAYE, AIDS levy and any other deductions you enter.

If you are paid in both currencies, ZIMRA's published rule is to use the USD tables and apportion the tax. We do that, converting the ZiG part at the exchange rate in the rate box.

Official sources

  • ZIMRA tax tables: PAYE tables for 1 January to 31 December 2026 (USD and ZWG).
  • ZIMRA PAYE guidance: order of calculation, AIDS levy, credits, mixed-currency rule, payment by the 10th.
  • NSSA contributions: 4.5% each, US$700 ceiling, gazetted quarterly, employees below 65.
  • Finance Act (No. 2) of 2024: raised the tax-free bonus limit from US$400 to US$700 (reported by MMM Law Firm). ZIMRA's PAYE Explained page still shows US$400, so ask your employer which limit it applies.
  • Standards Development Levy: Standards Development Fund Act [Chapter 14:19], section 3 (imposed on classes of employers specified by the Minister of Industry and Commerce, up to 0.5% of remuneration; optional in the employer calculator).
  • ZIMDEF rate and exemption (reported by M&J Consultants).
  • Reference exchange rate: Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe interbank rate (26.56 on 11 November 2026).

Every figure sits in one documented file, config.js, so it can be checked and updated quickly. The calculators are tested against the 2026 table figures and ZIMRA's published worked examples. Figures last verified: 18 November 2026.

What the calculators do not cover

  • Taxable benefits such as company cars, housing or loans.
  • Retrenchment and severance packages, share options, back pay or leave pay.
  • Part-year or mid-year changes of employer.
  • Legal limits on pension or retirement fund deductions, and the detail of the elderly concession.
  • APWCS and industry levies (NEC and similar), which depend on the employer, so you enter them yourself.
  • Pay periods other than monthly. Enter your monthly figure.
Spotted a number that looks out of date? Tax rules change. Compare with the ZIMRA tax tables and your payslip, and treat this site as an estimate.
